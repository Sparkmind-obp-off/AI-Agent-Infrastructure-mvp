## New methodological shift (Cycle 017)
Cycle 017 asked a structural rather than a screening question:
> Can we generate names from a sufficiently unexplored naming territory that naturally has lower collision density before expensive clearance research?

The generation pool was therefore split across five materially different structural territories (below), not one homogeneous style. The hypothesis under test: **vowel-initial and open-CV.CV.CV geometries (never dominant in Cycles 013–016) should exhibit measurably lower collision density than the compact 2-syllable and -yvex/-yn/-us corridors already exhausted.**

## Strategic learning
1. **The Cycle 016 diagnosis is empirically confirmed.** Vowel-initial and open-CV.CV.CV geometries (Territories A–B), which had NOT been systematically explored in Cycles 013–016, do **not** exhibit materially lower collision density than the compact 2-syllable or -yvex/-yn/-us corridors. Collision density in the 5–9-letter pronounceable-invented space is **structurally uniform at roughly 100% for public-web-visible identities**.
2. **The failure geometry is now dominated by "long-tail global identity", not any single language or industry.** In Cycle 017 the killing findings spanned: Bangladeshi pharma (Nulevi), Uyghur music vocabulary (Elbumi), Korean fandom slang (Bomuri), Kirundi grammar (Bomuri), Esperanto verbs (Ondumi), Romanian verbs (Denumi), KBBI Indonesian verbs (Uleni), Namibian tourism (Onduri), Nigerian villages (Ipomu), Indian fintech IPOs (Onemi), UK software LLCs (Ipabi), and Ruby developer-ecosystem packages (Erubi). No single-domain filter can suppress this — the collisions are structurally omnidirectional.
3. **Same-category (AI/agent/SaaS/infra) collision rate in Cycle 017: ≥ 4 of 31 (~13%)** (Enapi, Panobi, Rumobi, Ipabi). Consistent with Cycle 016's finding that AI-adjacent SaaS is now the dominant destroyer.
4. **Invention batches have reached terminal exhaustion.** Cycles 015 + 016 + 017 have destruction-tested a cumulative 100+ generated candidates from ≥13 structural families with **zero survivors**. Continuing to generate more single-word invented candidates has diminishing marginal probability of finding a survivor.

## Next-cycle trigger
Cycle 018 should **not** be another single-word invention batch. The strategically appropriate next moves, in order of decreasing marginal expected value:
1. **Registry-first search**: start from an authoritatively pre-cleared candidate list (USPTO / EUIPO available marks in Nice classes 9, 35, 42; WIPO Global Brand DB negative-space queries) and only test structural viability *after* legal-space viability is established.
2. **Compound / phrasal marks**: two-word or determinant-plus-noun constructions (à la "Vercel Ship", "Fly Machines", "Modal Sandbox") where the mark is the whole phrase, not a single invented lexeme. Distinctiveness is carried by the combination, and single-word saturation ceases to be the limiting factor.
3. **Broker-mediated acquisition**: engage a naming/domain broker to source a pre-existing but for-sale mark with a clean .com and USPTO/EUIPO history.
4. **Deferred naming**: continue MVP engineering under the "AI-Agent-Infrastructure-mvp" placeholder, and revisit master naming only when product-market fit narrows the semantic target (which shrinks the necessary search space).

Cycle 018 execution prompt should encode Option 1 or Option 2 explicitly and freeze single-word invented territory permanently.
