## 3. PERMANENT NAMING CONSTITUTION

Preserve the established target:

- premium;
- abstract;
- neutral;
- global;
- scalable;
- technology/infrastructure credible;
- enterprise-compatible;
- Indonesian + English pronounceable;
- reasonably recoverable spelling after hearing;
- low semantic baggage;
- extensible as a master brand.

Avoid obvious descriptive constructions such as:

`AI`, `Agent`, `Tech`, `Cloud`, `Digital`, `Labs`, `Systems`, `Platform`, `Automation`, `OS`, `Runtime`, `Stack`, `GPT`.

Do not repeat heavily exhausted phonetic families or previously rejected candidates.

Previously rejected candidates remain rejected unless materially new evidence changes their status.

## 4. NEW METHODOLOGY

Cycle 015 MUST materially differ from Cycles 001–014.

Do not simply create another batch of English-looking startup names.

Derive the new construction method from the historical failure map.

Prefer constructions with:

- clean consonant/vowel balance;
- compact spelling;
- strong first-hearing recognition;
- stable Indonesian pronunciation;
- stable English pronunciation;
- low surname resemblance;
- low place resemblance;
- low medical resemblance;
- low fantasy resemblance;
- low technical-descriptor resemblance;
- strong enterprise infrastructure credibility.

Document exactly why this method is different.

## 6. ONE-CANDIDATE DESTRUCTION LOOP

Test candidates one at a time.

For each candidate:

**GENERATE → PHONETIC → EXACT SEARCH → ROOT/SUBSTRING → WEB → COMPANY → SOFTWARE → DEVELOPER ECOSYSTEM → NEGATIVE ASSOCIATION → DOMAIN → REGISTRY → PRIMARY ASSOCIATION → INFRASTRUCTURE TEST → ADVERSARIAL TEST → DECIDE**

If it fails a hard gate:

**REJECTED — record exact evidence — move immediately to the next new candidate.**

Do not rescue a failed candidate.
Do not substitute a spelling variant as rescue.
Do not stop after the first failure.

Continue until a candidate survives or the evidence-supported candidate pool is exhausted.

## 7. PHONETIC GATE

For each candidate test:

- “Nama platform kami [NAME].”
- “Backend agent kami berjalan di [NAME].”
- “Gunakan [NAME] untuk membangun agent.”
- “Cari [NAME] di internet.”
- “Install [NAME] SDK.”

Record:

- Indonesian reading;
- English reading;
- alternate readings;
- hearing-to-spelling;
- telephone clarity;
- pronunciation drift.

Reject material ambiguity or problematic accidental meanings.

- fiction;

- registry conflicts;
- search ambiguity.
