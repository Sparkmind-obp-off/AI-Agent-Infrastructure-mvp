# AI Agent Infrastructure — Brand Naming Cycle 020 Summary

**Status:** NO BRAND LOCK  
**Cycle:** 020  
**Execution date:** 2026-09-21  
**Repository:** `Sparkmind-obp-off/AI-Agent-Infrastructure-mvp`  
**Method:** Strategic whole-mark screening  
**Decision state:** No strategic survivor; professional clearance is not triggered for a candidate.

## Executive conclusion

Cycle 020 produced **NO BRAND LOCK**. Eight candidates were researched across premium compact, distinctive real-lexical, and natural-compound lanes. None has a sufficiently clean, evidence-supported path to commercial differentiation as an AI-agent infrastructure master brand.

Caldrin, Avenor, Tervane, and Ravelin have direct or materially adjacent AI, agent, API, software, or infrastructure collisions. Selvara, Orvellan, and Slate Mere retain some brandability, but the observed C3/C5 evidence prevents them from qualifying as strategic survivors under the active standard. Cairnward has strong infrastructure lift but is already used for private AI, security, and agent-operations activity; its exact-mark occupancy is therefore material despite the unrelatedness of some individual uses.

This result is a strategic naming screen, not legal clearance, a registrability opinion, or a likelihood-of-confusion opinion.

## 3. Strategic methodology reset

The cycle replaced the former component-occupancy test with the following order:

1. Evaluate the whole mark.
2. Identify the primary association.
3. Assess category proximity and collision severity.
4. Test commercial distinctiveness and infrastructure lift.
5. Preserve C1/C2 candidates for investigation when no material blocker is present.
6. Reject C3/C4/C5 candidates unless evidence clearly supports a professionally reviewable whole-mark distinction.

The objective was not to find a name with no web occurrences. The objective was to find a name with a credible path to ownership, discoverability, and long-term differentiation.

## 6. Collision severity findings

The most important pattern was not incidental component occupancy. It was exact or near-exact identity in the same commercial neighborhood as the intended master brand.

**C5 / direct material conflict.** Caldrin, Tervane, and Ravelin each have exact active uses with direct AI, agent, API, security, fraud, cloud, SDK, or infrastructure relevance. Avenor also has a dense exact-mark collision set that includes agent orchestration and AI-native infrastructure.

**C3 / material category conflict.** Orvellan and Slate Mere do not show the same direct AI-agent infrastructure incumbent as Ravelin, but both have exact active uses and category-adjacent evidence that prevents a strategic survivor designation under the stated gate. Cairnward’s exact AI and security uses are sufficiently close to the intended category to classify the whole mark as material rather than incidental.

**C1/C2 evidence.** Unrelated company records, furniture, hospitality, games, ordinary dictionary meanings, personal names, and isolated package or repository results were documented as contextual evidence. They were not treated as terminal merely because they existed. In each final rejection, the decisive evidence was stronger: an exact AI/software/security use, an exact infrastructure-adjacent use, or a crowded technology stem combined with an exact whole-mark use.

## 7. Brandability and phonetics

The candidates generally passed the basic aesthetic test in isolation. Caldrin, Avenor, Tervane, Orvellan, Selvara, and Ravelin were compact and pronounceable enough to support a technology-company wordmark. Cairnward provided clear security semantics, and Slate Mere was visually legible as a compound.

The weaknesses were practical rather than purely aesthetic. Avenor has pronunciation and spelling neighbors such as Avenir, Avinor, and Avanor. Tervane is exposed to Tervan and Tervene near-matches. Orvellan can evoke Orwellian in an AI control or security context. Cairnward has a non-obvious English pronunciation for *cairn*. Slate Mere has predictable spelling but a weak, crowded Slate stem and a possible *mere/mare* sound variation. Selvara has plausible Selvara/Selvera variants. Ravelin is linguistically strong, but its exact incumbent is decisive.

No candidate produced an obvious obscene or materially embarrassing Indonesian meaning in the practical screen. Indonesian and English pronunciation findings remain screening judgments, not native-speaker testing or formal linguistic counsel.

## 8. Infrastructure-lift result

All eight candidates could be combined naturally with at least some of **Runtime, SDK, API, Gateway, Cloud, Console, Enterprise, Security, Observability,** and **Execution**. This confirms that infrastructure lift alone is not a sufficient differentiator.

The strongest lift belonged to Ravelin, Cairnward, and Caldrin, but each is weakened by exact incumbents in adjacent or overlapping infrastructure categories. Avenor and Tervane sound credible as platforms but are already used by AI and software businesses. Slate Mere’s product extensions are less natural because Slate already dominates the technology signal. Orvellan and Selvara remain linguistically workable but do not overcome their evidence-based category risks.

## 9. Trademark, domain, social, and ecosystem evidence

Public-web screening found active exact domains and commercial properties for several candidates. This is identity evidence, not a domain-availability conclusion. No registrar-level purchase or acquisition was attempted.

Developer-ecosystem checks surfaced material exact or adjacent signals for Caldrin, Avenor, and Ravelin, including an exact agent-orchestration repository, an exact AI-agent company, and Ravelin’s official API/SDK ecosystem. Some package and registry probes returned no exact result, but absence from a public endpoint is not proof of availability.

Official trademark portals were identified and their public search guidance was reviewed. Exact, fuzzy, phonetic, class-specific, status-verified searches were not completed across USPTO, EUIPO/TMview, WIPO, UK IPO, and Indonesia DJKI/PDKI. No candidate is legally clear, trademark safe, globally clear, or completely unused.

## 10. Adversarial findings

The strongest attack against Cycle 020 is that the research batch remained inside a highly saturated naming space. Even the candidates that looked invented or neutral acquired material risk once tested against AI agents, infrastructure, API, security, and developer ecosystems.

A CTO could plausibly confuse Caldrin, Avenor, Tervane, or Ravelin with existing technology businesses. A developer could encounter Avenor through an agent-orchestration repository or Ravelin through an API and SDK ecosystem. Search results for Slate Mere would be dominated by Slate technology identities. Cairnward would create a particularly direct association with private AI and enterprise resilience. These are evidence-based reasons not to promote the names to strategic-survivor status.

## 12. Professional-clearance requirements

Because no candidate was promoted, professional clearance is not yet recommended for a Cycle 020 finalist. If a future cycle produces a candidate without a material C3/C4/C5 signal, professional clearance should cover exact, fused, hyphenated, reversed, plural, phonetic, and component forms in the intended launch territories.

The search should include Nice classes 9, 35, 38, and 42 where relevant, with USPTO/TSDR, EUIPO/TMview, WIPO Global Brand Database, UK IPO, and DJKI/PDKI Indonesia coverage. It should also include company-name records, domains and DNS, social identities, GitHub, npm, PyPI, crates.io, Docker, MCP, app stores, common-law use, ownership, status, and opposition history.

## 13. Final status

**NO BRAND LOCK.**

Cycle 020 does not authorize public launch, filing, domain purchase, or product renaming. The repository placeholder remains the appropriate working identity until a later cycle produces a candidate with materially better evidence.

## 14. Strategic learning

The whole-mark reset corrected the earlier tendency to reject every candidate because one component appeared elsewhere. That correction was useful: unrelated uses were contextualized rather than automatically treated as terminal.

The reset did not produce a survivor because the strongest collisions were whole-mark or category-adjacent identities. The practical bottleneck remains the concentration of exact names in AI, agent orchestration, APIs, cloud, enterprise software, security, and developer tooling.

The next cycle should avoid repeating the same unconstrained compact-name batch. It should either begin with a registry-first negative-space process, use professionally sourced or brokered marks, or explore more distinctive natural compounds after a documented knockout screen. Creative investment should occur only after direct C3/C4/C5 signals have been ruled out at the screening level.

## 15. Next evidence-producing action

Generate a fresh batch under a changed strategy, then run a standardized knockout screen before developing a brand system. The screen should prioritize exact and fuzzy whole-mark searches in relevant classes and territories, with Indonesia included from the beginning. Candidates should be promoted to C1/C2 investigation only when no direct AI, agent, API, infrastructure, security, or materially adjacent collision is found.

Do not rescue Mettle Cove, Trace Orchard, Orchard, OrChat, or other terminal Cycle 015–019 candidates through spelling, reversal, punctuation, fusion, or category suffixes.
