# 5. REGISTRY-FIRST PRINCIPLE

Cycle 018 is **registry-first**, not search-engine-first.

Where authoritative public registry access is available, use it before investing deep research in a candidate.

Priority:

1. USPTO / TSDR or current official U.S. trademark search
2. EUIPO
3. WIPO Global Brand Database
4. UK IPO
5. DJKI / PDKI Indonesia
6. other authoritative national registries when relevant

Relevant Nice classes:
- Class 9;
- Class 35;
- Class 38;
- Class 42.

The objective is not to prove legal ownership.

The objective is to eliminate candidates with obvious existing marks before deep semantic research.

Evidence labels:

- VERIFIED
- SEARCH-OBSERVED
- PARTIAL
- UNVERIFIED
- REQUIRES PROFESSIONAL CLEARANCE

No registry access:
**UNVERIFIED**

No exact result:
does NOT mean legally clear.

# 6. CANDIDATE CONSTRUCTION

Generate a broad internal pool of:

**100–150 compound/phrasal candidates.**

At least 6 structural families.

## Family A — Infrastructure noun + action

Examples of semantic ingredients:
- fabric;
- rail;
- bridge;
- frame;
- layer;
- plane;
- lattice;
- relay;
- route;
- dispatch;
- trace;
- run.

Do not simply combine two generic words.

## Family B — Action + infrastructure

Examples:
- route;
- run;
- dispatch;
- execute;
- relay;
- trace;
- anchor;
- shape;
- build;
- bind.

Combined with:
- fabric;
- rail;
- field;
- frame;
- plane;
- layer;
- works;
- forge;
- yard.

## Family C — Abstract determinant + infrastructure noun

Use a meaningful modifier that creates a distinctive whole.

Avoid generic AI adjectives:
- smart;
- intelligent;
- autonomous;
- neural;
- cognitive;
- agentic;
- AI.

## Family D — One uncommon word + one infrastructure word

The first word may be:
- architectural;
- operational;
- physical;
- systems-oriented;
- metaphorical.

But the combined mark must not simply describe the product.

## Family E — Two-word phrasal marks

The phrase itself carries the identity.

Examples of structure only:
- [distinctive noun] [operational noun]
- [distinctive verb] [infrastructure noun]
- [metaphor] [execution concept]

Do NOT copy famous brands such as Vercel Ship, Fly Machines, Modal Sandbox, or other existing marks.

They are structural references only.

## Family F — Compound with controlled coined component

One component may be mildly coined, but the overall mark must remain:
- pronounceable;
- writable;
- semantically coherent;
- not another pseudo-Latin standalone invention.

---

# 14. PRIMARY ASSOCIATION

Classify:

**CLEAN**
- no meaningful dominant identity.

**MOSTLY CLEAN**
- minor unrelated noise but no dominant conflict.

**MIXED**
- multiple identities with no single dominant owner.

**DOMINATED**
- one strong unrelated identity dominates.

**CONFLICTING**
- multiple strong identities compete.

Only CLEAN / MOSTLY CLEAN candidates should normally advance to final destruction.

MIXED may advance only when the whole-mark distinctiveness is unusually strong and evidence warrants deeper inspection.

# 16. ADVERSARIAL DESTRUCTION

For each serious candidate ask:

1. What single fact makes the whole mark unusable?
2. Is the whole phrase already a known brand?
3. Is there an exact trademark in relevant classes?
4. Is there a same-category company?
5. Is there a developer ecosystem identity?
6. Is the combination descriptive/generic?
7. Is one component misleading?
8. Is the phrase politically/religiously/fantasy coded?
9. Is there a surname/place conflict that dominates hearing?
10. Is there medical/pharma association?
11. Is there major-language negative meaning?
12. Is it hard to hear or spell?
13. Does the whole phrase sound like a feature rather than a master brand?
14. Does it remain credible if AI becomes commodity infrastructure?
15. Would an enterprise CTO reasonably accept the name as an infrastructure company?

Terminal answer:
**REJECTED**

Do not rescue.

# 17. SURVIVOR STANDARD

A candidate is a **SURVIVOR** only when:

- no obvious authoritative registry conflict has been found;
- no dominant same-category company identity has been found;
- no material software/developer collision has been found;
- no material Indonesian/cross-lingual issue has been found;
- phonetic test passes;
- whole-mark is distinctive enough to function as a master brand;
- infrastructure lift passes;
- adversarial destruction does not reveal a terminal issue.

Even then:

**SURVIVOR — PROFESSIONAL CLEARANCE REQUIRED**

not “legally clear”.
