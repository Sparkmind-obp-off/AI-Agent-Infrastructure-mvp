## 9. Cross-Language Findings

### 9.1 UMTRAYEN

- **English:** no dictionary meaning. Pig-Latin risk: `Umtray` appears as the Pig-Latin encoding of **"trump"** in the widely-shared meme phrase "Uckfay Umtray" (= "Fuck Trump"), used on public social feeds. Anyone Pig-Latin-literate reads `UMTRAY` as an encoded political slur. **HIDDEN MEANING — POLITICAL/SLANG.**
- **German / Dutch:** the prefix `um-` is a productive verb-particle. `Umtray-` sits near `umtragen` / `umtreiben` shapes; not identical but the ear may complete it as a verb.
- **Spanish / Portuguese:** `umtrajen` surfaces as a rare Colombian-Spanish social-media form (likely a colloquial contraction of `um(a) trajen` or a misspelling); one Facebook thread uses it in a politically inflected sentence. Low but non-zero adjacency.
- **Indonesian:** `um-` is not a native prefix pattern; the word reads as foreign; `trayen` has no ID meaning; no vulgar/religious reading observed.
- **French / Italian / Nordic (NO/SE/FI/EE/LV/LT) / Arabic / Hindi / Bengali / Gujarati / Tamil / Telugu / Kannada / JP / KR / Mandarin:** no dictionary hit observed. Not claimed exhaustive.

### 9.2 TRUNVOLD

- **Norwegian (Bokmål/Nynorsk) and Danish:** `vold` is a live, high-frequency common noun meaning **violence / force / assault and battery** (also archaic "meadow" and "rampart"). A Nordic reader parses an unfamiliar `Trun-vold` as either a habitational surname (like Tronvold) or a compound whose second element is the modern word for violence. **HIDDEN MEANING — NORDIC LEXICAL.**

## 16. Adversarial Destruction

**Failure-mode question: what single finding makes each name unusable as master brand?**

### 16.1 UMTRAYEN
- The single killing finding is the **hidden-meaning + material-root double-hit**:
  1. `UMTRAY` (the material root, 6/8 characters) is a documented, cited technical identifier for a GSM-BTS software-defined-radio testbed in peer-reviewed telecom-security literature. A brand whose infrastructure/developer audience overlaps mobile-networking or SDR communities will encounter this identifier and read `UMTRAYEN` as a related project. This is a material-root collision inside an adjacent-goods corridor (Class 9 / Class 42).
  2. `UMTRAY` is also the Pig-Latin encoding of **"trump"**, actively used on public social feeds in the phrase "Uckfay Umtray." Any Pig-Latin-literate audience — a large slice of English-speaking developers and marketers — reads the substring immediately as an encoded political slur. This is not defeatable through logo, tagline, or launch narrative.
- Failure directly tested: verified against academic literature (River Publishers journal article on end-to-end mobile-communication security testbed) and against public social-media Pig-Latin usage.
- **Outcome: UMTRAYEN REJECTED.**

### 16.2 TRUNVOLD
- The single killing finding is the **surname-bleed + Nordic-lexical double-hit**:
  1. `Trunvold` is a living Norwegian/Norwegian-American family surname. Confirmed bearers on public record include a Watertown, South Dakota family; a Savage, Minnesota family (Tres Trunvold, race results 2007–2008); an Iowa high-school athlete (Kirsten Trunvold, Crestwood 2021–22); and historical Norwegian church-book entries 1812–1938 (Iversd Trunvold Sigri, Kleve Sogn) archived on geneanet.org. The surname is structurally identical to the confirmed Norwegian habitational pattern of `Tronvold`, `Trondvold`, `Brandvold`, `Bratvold` (all documented Norwegian surnames formed as `X + voll/vold` meaning "farm in a grassy field"). This is the exact surname-density collision layer Cycle 010's learning identified as a systemic issue.
  2. In modern Norwegian Bokmål/Nynorsk and Danish, `vold` is a high-frequency common noun meaning **violence, force, assault and battery**, with an entire family of productive compounds (`voldtekt` rape, `voldsmand` violent offender, `voldsom` violent, `hustruvold` domestic violence, `politivold` police brutality, `voldsforbryder` violent criminal, `voldsscene` violent scene, `voldsfilm` violent film). Any Nordic-market reader who does not immediately parse `Trunvold` as an existing family name will parse the second morpheme as the modern word for violence. The archaic "meadow" reading is not the default modern read.
- Failure directly tested: verified against Wiktionary Bokmål/Danish/Nynorsk entries for `vold`, against Norwegian surname corpora (forebears.io reachable via cache-adjacent references; ancestry.com Tronvold entry), and against Norwegian church-record indices.

- **Outcome: TRUNVOLD REJECTED.**

## 20. Strategic Learning

1. **The two-candidate shortlist did not survive a candidate-specific adversarial cycle.** Cycle 012 correctly labelled the shortlist as "not legally safe / not cleared." Cycle 013 confirms the shortlist was also brittle at the constitutional neutrality gate the moment a second, more targeted adversarial pass was run on each name individually.

3. **The `-vold` suffix is a Nordic-market landmine.** Any Nordic-shaped invented name whose second morpheme is `-vold` inherits the modern "violence" reading regardless of surname parallels. The surname corridor (Tronvold, Brandvold, Bratvold) is safe *because* those are already-registered family names read as habitational — an invented lookalike inherits the surname bleed without inheriting the safety.
4. **Spelling discipline between prompt and history matters.** The Cycle 013 prompt named `TRUNVOLD`; Cycle 012 shortlisted `TRUNVELD`. Silently equating the two would have been a fabrication. This spelling gap should be resolved at the prompt-authoring layer before Cycle 014.
5. **Cycle 010's saturation diagnosis is reconfirmed.** Two hand-picked survivors from a disciplined cycle still fail a candidate-specific adversarial audit. The next constructive move is not another invention batch.
