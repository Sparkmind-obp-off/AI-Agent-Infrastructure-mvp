## 5. CYCLE 003 STRUCTURAL SHIFT

Cycle 003 must change the naming search space.

Use BOTH of these primary construction directions:

### Direction A — Linguistic corridor

Explore root material outside the saturated English/Latin/Greek corridor.

Priority families:

1. **Austronesian**
   - Javanese
   - Sundanese
   - Minangkabau
   - broader Malayic/Austronesian vocabulary where culturally and linguistically defensible

2. **Old Norse / Icelandic**
   - only where the root is genuinely documented
   - avoid famous mythology already saturated in technology

3. **Finnish / Estonian**
   - only where pronunciation can remain practical for Indonesian and international users

4. **Other low-collision linguistic corridors**
   - may be proposed if evidence shows lower category saturation
   - must explain why the corridor is appropriate and what cultural/linguistic risks exist

Do NOT exploit a language merely because it is obscure.

For every non-Indonesian root, document:

- actual source language
- literal meaning
- pronunciation
- transliteration
- grammatical status where relevant
- cultural context
- whether the root is common, archaic, slang, sacred, personal-name-derived, geographic, or otherwise sensitive

### Direction B — Multi-morpheme construction

Test compact two-morpheme constructions rather than single dictionary nouns.

Examples of construction logic ONLY:

- root + root
- root + altered phonetic connector
- two short semantic roots
- culturally legitimate compound
- coined compound derived from documented roots

Do NOT simply glue random English words together.

Do NOT use generic startup suffixes such as `-ix`, `-yn`, `-yx`, `-ryn`, `-trin`, `-stra`, `-adin`, `-ell`, `-oc`, `-ris`, `-tin`.

The construction must have a defensible linguistic or phonotactic rationale.
