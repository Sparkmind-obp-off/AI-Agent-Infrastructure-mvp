## 3. New User Naming Constitution (Cycle 004)
The project owner introduced a materially new brand-direction constraint:

> The next naming search must prioritize a genuinely neutral, non-religious,
> non-mythological, non-fantasy, premium, elegant, modern, highly brandable
> identity.

Operationalized as hard gates:
- Neutrality (no religion / mythology / deity / spiritual / occult /
  fantasy / monster / creature / political / extremist / dominant
  personal-name / dominant geographic association).
- Brandability elevated to a first-class gate (premium + elegant +
  modern + memorable + pronounceable in Indonesian and English).
- Coined marks with no dictionary meaning are ACCEPTABLE.
- Obscurity is NOT a substitute for neutrality.
- Do not chase low search volume for its own sake.

## 10. Brandability Findings

  vowel-final coined marks with clean tech phonemes (the "Kentrova /
  Coretava / Novaren / Kernova" cluster) are **already colonized**
  by pharma, active AI-SaaS, fashion, consumer, or Cyprus/Belgian
  corporate registries — even when they carry zero literal meaning.
- The Italian-Latin diminutive-suffix corridor (-ella / -ita / -etta /
  -ora / -ova) systematically drifts perception toward fashion / beauty
  / wellness / feminine personal name, weakening infrastructure
  credibility even when a candidate is search-clean.
- Short 2-syllable modern-tech coined marks (Vercel / Stripe /
  Ramp / Notion / Figma pattern) were under-explored due to the
  Cycle 004 avoid-suffix list (-ix, -yn, -trin, -oc, -tin, etc.).
  This is a design gap in Cycle 004's construction filter.
- **Brandability is the binding constraint of Cycle 004**, and the
  reason the funnel produced zero finalists rather than 1–3.

## 13. Strategic Learning
1. **Neutrality-first + brandability-first is the right constitution.**
   It correctly rejected all Cycle 003 finalists (which each carried
   a non-neutral aesthetic — Nordic-saga, seasonal-landscape, fantasy,
   or Sanskrit-wellness) and correctly prevented a false-positive
   Cycle 004 lock on Kernvella.

2. **The invented 3-syllable vowel-final "modern tech" corridor is now
   as saturated as the English/Latin/Greek and Nordic/Finnic
   corridors** studied in Cycles 001–003. Empirically:
   - Pharma trademarks colonize the -venta/-vela/-trela endings
     (Torventa/Corvela/Vantrela were all live pharmaceutical brands).
   - Cyprus/Belgian/Spanish corporate registries hold dormant LLCs
     across almost every phonetically pleasant coined mark.
   - Fictional-universe pollution (FFXVI Norvent, WoW Corvenna, Far
     Cry 6 Marlento) is not limited to fantasy-styled names — even
     neutral coined marks get consumed as fictional-location names.

3. **The naming pressure is now three-dimensional**:
   - Category dimension (Cycle 001–002 finding): saturated.
   - Corridor dimension (Cycle 003 finding): partially open in
     Nordic/Finnic compounds but produces mythology-adjacent aesthetics
     that fail Cycle 004's neutrality constitution.
   - Architecture dimension (Cycle 004 finding): coined 3-syllable
     marks with neutral phonemes are also saturated by pharma,
     fashion, and dormant corporate registries.

4. **Brand-architecture pivot may be necessary.** The next viable
   moves are not more coining inside the same architecture; they are
   architecture changes:
   - Short 2-syllable ≤6-letter coined marks (Vercel / Stripe / Ramp /
     Figma template) — under-explored in Cycle 004; explicitly excluded
     by the -ix/-yn/-oc suffix ban.
   - Two-word / phrasal master brands (e.g., "Runtime Loop",
     "Agent Foundry", "Kernel Cove", "Substrate Cloud") — changes the
     collision surface entirely.
   - Real-English short lexical marks (Notion/Linear/Retool pattern) —
     conflict-heavy but has produced the strongest current tech brands.
   - Real-Latin short lexical marks with clear infra semantics
     (Substrate, Cadence, Anchor, Fabric) — must survive Cycle 001/002
     collision axes.

5. **Cycle 004's neutrality constitution should persist** across all
   future cycles — it is a durable rule, not a one-cycle
   experiment. The failure to lock a brand in Cycle 004 is a
   **honest negative result**, not a reason to relax the constitution.
