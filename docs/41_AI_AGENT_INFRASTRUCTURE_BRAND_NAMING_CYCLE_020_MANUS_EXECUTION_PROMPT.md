## 4. CYCLE 020 METHODOLOGY RESET

Use three independent naming lanes.

### LANE A — LOW-SATURATION REAL WORDS

Find real, attested words that satisfy all of:

- simple
- 4–9 letters preferred
- 1–3 syllables preferred
- easy Indonesian pronunciation
- easy English pronunciation
- spelling recoverable from hearing
- neutral meaning
- not a common personal name
- not geography
- not medical/pharma
- not consumer-product dominated
- not technical/descriptive
- low commercial saturation
- credible as a parent company

Do NOT simply list famous dictionary words.

Prioritize words with:
- secondary/less common but positive meanings
- structural, relational, spatial, or abstract meanings
- low visual/semantic baggage
- no dominant modern software identity

Target: at least 150 raw candidates.

### LANE B — NATURAL LEXICAL COMPOUNDS

Use only existing words whose combination creates a coherent but uncommon whole mark.

Requirements:
- each component must be individually low-risk
- neither component may dominate the category
- whole phrase must have a distinct commercial reading
- avoid place-name/hospitality/property patterns
- avoid art/media/architecture/product-name patterns
- avoid obvious technical nouns
- avoid ordinary startup compounds

Target: at least 100 raw candidates.

### LANE C — ATTESTED LEXICAL-BRIDGE NAMES

This is the methodology change.

A candidate may be a compact one-word form ONLY if its linguistic structure is naturally explainable from real lexical material.

Accept:
- a genuine compound used in a real language;
- an established lexical form with a documented meaning;
- a natural historical/archaic lexical form;
- a short lexical form whose morphemes are independently meaningful and whose pronunciation is stable.

Reject:
- random CV invention;
- invented pseudo-Latin;
- pseudo-Nordic;
- pseudo-Germanic;
- surname imitation;
- pharma morphology;
- fantasy morphology;
- forced Q/X/Z;
- arbitrary vowel substitution;
- one-letter mutation of an existing candidate.

The purpose of Lane C is NOT to invent startup strings.
The purpose is to discover natural lexical forms that are commercially under-occupied.

Target: at least 100 raw candidates.

## 10. COMPONENT-COLLISION RULE

Cycle 019 demonstrated that a whole phrase can look clean while both components are already occupied.

Therefore:

For every multi-word candidate, test EACH component independently.

For every one-word candidate, test obvious morphemes and phonetic neighbors.

A candidate is terminally rejected if:
- a component is a dominant same-category AI/infrastructure identity;
- a component has a major developer ecosystem identity;
- a component creates a strong technology-mark association;
- the whole phrase is likely to be interpreted as an existing product/company family.

Do not wait for an exact whole-mark collision.
