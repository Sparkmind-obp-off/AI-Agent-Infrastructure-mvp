## 3. CYCLE 020 NAMING OBJECTIVE

Find names that are:

- premium;
- elegant;
- modern;
- simple;
- memorable;
- brandable;
- neutral;
- global;
- easy to pronounce;
- reasonably recoverable from hearing;
- credible as a technology company;
- credible as infrastructure;
- broad enough for a 10+ year platform;
- distinct enough to justify professional clearance.

The name does NOT need to be globally unused.

The name does NOT need every component to be unused.

The name does NOT need an exact .com.

The name DOES need a credible path to commercial differentiation and ownership.

Use the spirit of the project's established master-brand naming style — compact, confident, abstract, premium — without copying any existing brand.

---

## 4. GENERATION LANES

Use three lanes, but do not force artificial candidate counts.

### Lane A — Premium Compact Forms

Generate compact names in the general territory of:

- Kaervax;
- Kaivost;
- Calven-style constructions.

These are style references only.

Requirements:

- 5–9 letters preferred;
- 1–3 syllables;
- stable pronunciation;
- simple visual form;
- no obvious technical suffix;
- no fantasy/surname/pharma morphology;
- no forced Q/X/Z;
- no random CV strings.

### Lane B — Distinctive Real-Lexical Names

Use uncommon real words or lexical material when:

- the whole word has manageable commercial saturation;
- the primary association is not dominated;
- the meaning is neutral or positively usable;
- the word can become a master brand rather than a feature.

### Lane C — Natural Compounds / Adapted Forms

Use uncommon but natural combinations or linguistically explainable forms.

The whole mark must feel intentional and brandable.

Do not create ordinary startup compounds such as AI + noun, Agent + noun, Tech + noun, or Cloud + noun.

## 6. COLLISION SEVERITY — REQUIRED

For every observed collision classify:

### C0 — NO MATERIAL COLLISION
Proceed.

### C1 — INCIDENTAL / UNRELATED
Document. Do not automatically reject.

### C2 — ADJACENT / COMPONENT RISK
Investigate strength, category, market reach, similarity, and perception. Do not automatically reject.

### C3 — MATERIAL CATEGORY CONFLICT
Same or closely related AI/software/infrastructure identity. Normally reject unless the whole mark is clearly separable and professionally reviewable.

### C4 — DOMINANT / CONFUSING IDENTITY
Existing identity would likely dominate or confuse the proposed brand. Reject.

### C5 — PROFESSIONAL / LEGAL BLOCK
Authoritative or professional evidence identifies a material barrier. Reject.

**C1/C2 are investigation states, not terminal states.**

---

## 7. WHOLE-MARK TEST

For each serious candidate:

1. Search the exact whole mark.
2. Search fused/hyphenated/plural forms.
3. Search phonetic near-matches.
4. Search company/software/platform/AI/agent/infrastructure.
5. Search GitHub, npm, PyPI, crates.io, Docker, MCP.
6. Search domains and social.
7. Search trademark signals.
8. Determine primary association.
9. Determine whether the whole mark remains commercially distinct.

For multi-word names, inspect components, but do not make component occupancy terminal by itself.

For one-word names, inspect obvious morphemes and homophones, but do not reject substring matches automatically.

---

## 8. BRANDABILITY TEST

Every serious candidate must answer YES or NO to:

- Does it look good as a standalone wordmark?
- Can it be said naturally in Indonesian?
- Can it be said naturally in English?
- Can someone reasonably type it after hearing it?
- Does it feel premium?
- Does it feel modern?
- Does it avoid being trapped in one feature?
- Could it be the name of a serious technology company?
- Could it become an ecosystem umbrella?
- Could it remain credible if AI becomes commodity infrastructure?

Reject only for material failures.

## 10. LANGUAGE + PHONETICS

Screen Indonesian and English first.

Check:

- pronunciation stability;
- spelling recovery;
- obvious homophones;
- embarrassing meanings;
- person-name dominance;
- geography dominance;
- slang/offensive meanings.

Broader multilingual checks are required for finalists where practical.

Minor accent differences are NOT terminal.

Severe ambiguity or an occupied technology homophone can be terminal.

---

## 11. DOMAIN + SOCIAL

Check:

- .com;
- relevant Indonesian domain;
- practical alternatives;
- active owners;
- aftermarket signals;
- major social identities.

Classify separately:

**identity conflict**  
vs.  
**handle/domain availability**.

An unavailable handle alone is not a terminal collision.

An unavailable .com alone is not a terminal collision.

Do not purchase assets.

---

## 12. TRADEMARK PRE-SCREEN

Where accessible, inspect:

- DJKI/PDKI;
- USPTO;
- EUIPO;
- WIPO;
- UK IPO.

Prioritize:

- Class 9;
- Class 35 where relevant;
- Class 38 where relevant;
- Class 42.

Record evidence.

Never claim:

- legally safe;
- trademark safe;
- globally clear;
- completely unused.

Professional review remains separate.

---

## 13. ADVERSARIAL DESTRUCTION

For each finalist ask:

1. What is the strongest existing identity?
2. Is it same-category?
3. Would a CTO confuse it?
4. Would a developer mishear it?
5. Would search results be dominated?
6. Is there a serious trademark signal?
7. Is the negative meaning material?
8. Is the domain situation commercially workable?
9. Does it still work in ten years?
10. Does it work beyond today's AI terminology?
11. Does it support infrastructure architecture?
12. Does the whole mark remain distinguishable even if components have unrelated uses?

The question is:

> **Is there a material evidence-based reason NOT to use this whole mark for AI-agent infrastructure?**

Not:

> “Can I find any occurrence of this word online?”

---

## 14. SURVIVOR STATUS

If a candidate survives internal research:

**STRATEGIC SURVIVOR — PROFESSIONAL CLEARANCE REQUIRED**

This means:

- research is sufficiently positive;
- no C3/C4/C5 blocker has been identified;
- the brand is strong enough to justify professional review.

It does NOT mean legally cleared.

Do not keep generating candidates merely because the exact .com or every social handle is unavailable.

Do not force a survivor if material evidence says reject.
