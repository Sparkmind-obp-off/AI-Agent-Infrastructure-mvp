# Free Tier First Policy

A provider is MVP-eligible only when:
- the API can actually be called;
- the free allowance is sufficient for the intended test;
- a paid plan is not mandatory for the capability;
- unavoidable billing requirements are documented;
- limits and reset behavior are known.

Cloudflare wins only when all of those conditions are true.

A feature that exists in Cloudflare but requires Workers Paid is not considered a free MVP capability.

E2B is locked for sandbox execution because the MVP needs an isolated execution provider and E2B offers an accessible hobby/free entry point. Its one-time credit is treated as an MVP resource, not as a promise of unlimited free production execution.
