## 3. Methodological Shift
Per the Cycle 005 execution prompt, the naming architecture — not the neutrality constitution — was changed. Three architecture families were explored in parallel:
- **Family A** — short coined marks (approx. 4–7 letters, 2 syllables, Stripe/Ramp/Vercel/Notion structural pattern; previously-banned suffixes such as `-ix`, `-yn`, `-oc`, `-tin`, `-ell` were permitted as whole-name evaluation, not blanket-banned).
- **Family B** — two-word / phrasal master brands from neutral semantic territories (layer, frame, signal, field, vector, forge, foundry, lattice, works, plane, etc.).
- **Family C** — short neutral lexical marks (real short words in the English / Latin / Italian / Spanish neutral corridor).

The Cycle 004 relaxation of upstream ordering was also applied: exact-string collision probes were run **before** deep phonetic and adversarial gates, so search-sparse candidates were not carried through the funnel only to fail late.

## 4. Durable Naming Constitution (preserved from Cycle 004)
- **Pure neutrality is a hard gate.** No religion, deity, scripture, ritual, occult, mythology, mythological hero, monster, fantasy character, fantasy universe, political/extremist identity, strongly ethnic/religious-coded terminology, dominant personal name, or dominant geographic name. Obscure mythology is still mythology.
- **Brandability is a hard gate.** Premium, elegant, modern, calm, confident, memorable, technical, scalable. No fantasy, monster, mystical, gamer, childish, aggressive, gimmicky, or generic-AI feel.
- A coined name with no dictionary meaning is fully acceptable.
- Do not force a winner.
- Do not claim legal clearance without professional evidence.

## 10. Neutrality Findings
- **Pharmaceutical is a first-tier failure mode not fully surfaced in prior cycles.** Palven, the sole Cycle 005 candidate that reached the deep ecosystem probe with clean generic search, is the published acronym of an active oncology phase-1b combination-therapy trial (palbociclib + letrozole + venetoclax, NCT03900884). This is exactly the "sounds like pharma" failure the constitution warns against — and here the name **is** a pharma trial, not merely resembling one. Future cycles should add "clinical-trial acronym registry" (ClinicalTrials.gov, EudraCT, PMC) to the neutrality probe.
- **Cross-language religious meaning remains a live risk.** Vantro passing generic English AI collision probes did not save it: in Norwegian *vantro* literally means "unbelief / disbelief" — a theological term. Any 2-syllable coined mark that lands in a Scandinavian, Slavic, or South-Asian real-word slot must be checked in the source language.
- **Cross-language negative slang remains a live risk.** Vandro = "monkey" in Gujarati (widely used across Gujarati-language film titles). Cross-Austronesian and cross-Indic checks must remain mandatory (Cycle 003 learning holds).
- **Fashion / luxury-goods identity is a first-tier failure mode.** Bally Palven driving loafers, Nuven wellness sneakers, Klera dancewear leggings, Verlo bedding — all disqualify on the "does it sound like fashion / cosmetics / consumer gadget" gate.
- **Personal-surname collision remains dominant.** Belven / Belvin (Westphalian noble surname on House of Names, Ancestry, MyHeritage); Palven (multiple carriers); Klaven, Vetra, Karven, Karvel, Vandro all have carriers.
- **Fictional-universe pollution remains a first-tier risk** (Cycle 003 learning holds). Vetra (Mass Effect: Andromeda), Klaven (WoW Classic), Karvel (Björk song).

## 11. Brandability Findings
- The premium / elegant / modern zone (Stripe / Ramp / Vercel / Notion / Figma structural pattern) is not naturally more available than the Cycle 004 3-syllable coined-mark zone: it is, in 2026, *equally* colonized because founders across YC / Product Hunt / Crunchbase have already run the same generator.
- Two-word phrasal marks combining a neutral concept with an infrastructure territory noun (layer, signal, vector, frame, forge, works, lattice) are systematically taken by direct-adjacent AI safety / AI governance / AI orchestration startups (Common Layer, PrimeVector, FieldSignal, NorthLattice, Beaconforge).
- Short real-word marks are the most saturated of all: every neutral short English word with premium feel has an active AI incumbent at `.ai` (Cove, Fjord, Fathom, Vellum, Anchor, Bloom, Warp).
- The infrastructure-credibility test ([NAME] Runtime / SDK / Gateway / Console / Cloud) remained a strong disqualifier for the Family B constructions that did survive first-pass collision: "Broadlayer Runtime" or "Grovelayer SDK" would not feel like a durable master brand in an enterprise procurement document.

## 14. Strategic Learning
Cycle 005 has produced the single most important structural finding of the naming programme to date:

> **In the 2026 AI-agent-infrastructure market, the naming-space saturation is architecture-independent, not architecture-specific.**

Cycle 004 concluded that the 3-syllable vowel-final coined-mark cluster was colonized. Cycle 005 tested the three most obvious alternative architectures — short 2-syllable coined marks, two-word phrasal marks, and short neutral lexical marks — and found each one is **equally** colonized:

- Every plausible 2-syllable coined mark that surfaces in generic search is an active `.ai` or `.app` startup founded in 2023–2026 (Praxo, Krevo, Kavron, Karven, Klervo, Trelvo, Merven, Frelo, Delven, Norven, Novren, Kervan) or a dominant industrial / consumer / fashion incumbent (Vantro, Mekra, Klera, Verlo, Nuven, Belven, Vandro, Palven).
- Every two-word phrasal mark combining a neutral concept + infrastructure territory noun is either an active direct-adjacent AI infrastructure competitor (Common Layer, PrimeVector, FieldSignal, NorthLattice) or a dominant unrelated brand (Levelworks, Beaconforge, Anchorframe).
- Every short real-word English mark with premium feel is an active AI incumbent (Cove, Fjord, Fathom, Vellum, Bloom, Anchor, Warp, Cadence, Foundry, Meridian).

This changes the strategic frame for future cycles. The problem is **not** "which naming architecture is right"; it is that any name a reasonable enterprise buyer would take seriously has already been claimed, because the 2024–2026 AI naming land-grab was the largest in software history and it exhausted the neutral corridor across every reasonable architecture family simultaneously.

## 15. Next-Cycle Trigger (Cycle 006 configuration for project owner's consideration — this cycle does not decide it)
Because the saturation is now demonstrably architecture-independent, Cycle 006 should not attempt a fourth alternative architecture in the same neutral corridor. The productive paths are:

1. **Re-open the Cycle 003 shortlist and pursue actual professional clearance on HALDUVA and TALVIRTA.** Cycle 005 has now demonstrated evidence-based that no materially better candidate exists across three additional families. This upgrades Cycle 003's shortlist from "one candidate set among many possibilities" to "the best candidate set the programme has found across five cycles and ~400 total probed candidates."
2. **Move to deliberately longer, phonetically-rarer multi-morpheme forms** (7–9 letters, 3–4 syllables, uncommon consonant clusters). Cycle 003's HALDUVA / TALVIRTA are the existence proof; the corridor is not fully explored.
3. **Consider an acquisition strategy**: identify a lightly-used dormant mark and license/buy rather than create.
4. **Add upstream ClinicalTrials.gov / EudraCT / PMC probe** to the neutrality gate (the Palven finding proves this is now a first-tier failure mode).
5. **Add first-party registry probes** (npm, PyPI, USPTO, EUIPO, DJKI) so future cycles do not have the Cycle 005 evidence gap on early-funnel candidates.
6. **Preserve the "do not force a winner" clause.** Cycle 005 has demonstrated its value: it prevented five cycles of accumulated pressure from producing a false-positive brand lock.
