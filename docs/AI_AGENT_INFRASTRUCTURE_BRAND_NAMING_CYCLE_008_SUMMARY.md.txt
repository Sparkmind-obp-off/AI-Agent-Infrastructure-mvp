## 28. Lessons Learned
1. The AI/software commercial namespace is saturated across ALL short-to-medium
   coined-mark corridors tested this cycle: 8 of 12 (67%) deep-probed
   candidates showed dominant existing collisions.
2. Long candidates (7–9 letters) do not escape collision; they often collide
   with pharmaceutical brands or industrial company names in Eastern
   European / Russian / South Asian markets that English-native searches
   rarely surface early.
3. Two adversarial mechanisms specifically dominated this cycle:
   (a) pharma brand adjacency — AXEN pharmaceutical family is a live
       trans-regional brand; ANY candidate with an `Axen-` opening cannot
       be shipped without deep pharma clearance.
   (b) fantasy/gaming namespace poisoning — even obscure D&D character
       registries and homebrew game worlds surface via SEO on unique-looking
       coined strings; Quenlox demonstrates this.
4. AI-generated "fake-artist" streaming playlists (e.g. "Clara Wren Relax")
   are now systematically polluting coined-name namespace on Amazon Music,
   Apple Music, Spotify, and YouTube. This is a NEW collision class that did
   not exist in earlier cycles. Any coined candidate must be checked against
   DSP metadata going forward.
5. `Nyx-` openings deceptively common in gaming handles (League of Legends,
   Path of Exile) AND carry Greek mythological weight — do not use.
6. Hard-consonant openings (Q-, Qr-, Zy-, Kr-) did open under-explored
   corridor as intended, but did not deliver a survivor. This confirms the
   corridor is not "safer" — it just moves the collision surface.
