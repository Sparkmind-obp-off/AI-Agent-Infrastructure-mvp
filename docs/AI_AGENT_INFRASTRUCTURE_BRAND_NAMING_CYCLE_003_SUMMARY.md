## 10. Strategic Learning (Cycle 003)

1. **The Nordic/Finnic/Estonian corridor is materially less saturated than the
   English/Latin/Greek corridor** — genuine clearance gaps exist at the invented
   multi-morpheme compound level.
2. **But single Nordic/Finnic roots are already occupied:** Karsa, Talvora, Solmara,
   Kesto, Punos, Skerra, Hovda, Vindarra all collide. Cycle 002's diagnosis extends
   into 2026: pressure on non-English roots is rising.
3. **Personal-surname collision is now the dominant secondary failure mode.**
   Nordic/Finnic invented forms frequently coincide with real surnames (Hovda,
   Skerra, Karsanova, Vindraya). Must be an upstream gate, not a downstream
   observation.
4. **Fictional-universe pollution is now a first-tier risk** (Kestora / Bionicle;
   Halluma / Invincible; Yggdra/Bifrost / Marvel). Fandom search dominates
   primary association (§16) even when the fictional entity is minor.
5. **Cross-Austronesian negative-meaning check is mandatory, not optional.**
   Tanirava passed all English + Indonesian filters but was killed by Malagasy
   *rava* = "ruined". The Austronesian family is broader than Indonesian.
6. **Invented 3-syllable compounds with clean vowel-final endings and no
   consonant clusters** (Halduva, Talvirta) are the current best-performing
   architecture inside the Cycle 003 corridor.
