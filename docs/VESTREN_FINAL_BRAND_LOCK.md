# VESTREN — FINAL BRAND LOCK

## 1. Status

**FINAL BRAND LOCK — VESTREN**

Decision date: 2026-09-21  
Repository: Sparkmind-obp-off/AI-Agent-Infrastructure-mvp  
Scope: AI Agent Infrastructure / agent runtime / SDK / API / gateway / cloud / execution / developer platform.

VESTREN is the approved master brand for this project. Naming exploration is closed.

This internal brand lock does **not** represent a legal opinion or guarantee of trademark registration. Formal legal clearance, filing strategy, and jurisdiction-specific counsel remain separate professional workstreams.

## 2. Decision basis

Vestren passed the project's strategic naming standard after Cycle 020 and subsequent owner-led clearance checks.

The decision is based on the **whole mark**, intended category, evidence of conflicting use, linguistic usability, brand architecture, and infrastructure extensibility—not on an unrealistic requirement that the word be unused everywhere on the internet.

The owner reports having checked:

- DJKI Indonesia — trademark and relevant Nice classes 9/35/38/42
- USPTO — VESTREN and confusingly similar VESTR* marks
- EUIPO
- WIPO Global Brand Database
- UK IPO
- Corporate registries in Indonesia and relevant target jurisdictions
- Actual domain/social ownership
- Instagram/social occurrences, including unrelated fashion use

No material same-category blocker was identified in those checks.

Unrelated uses of the string/name, including fashion or historical/personal-name occurrences, are not by themselves grounds to reopen naming. They must be recorded as context only unless evidence establishes a material legal or commercial conflict.

## 3. Brand identity

**Master brand:** VESTREN

Vestren is an invented/brandable technology mark selected for:

- premium and modern character
- compact 7-letter form
- strong visual silhouette
- relatively stable Indonesian/English pronunciation
- easy spelling recovery
- non-descriptive character
- infrastructure-grade extensibility
- suitability for global technical positioning

Preferred conceptual reading: a serious infrastructure technology company, not a consumer fashion label, fantasy universe, pharmaceutical product, or generic AI assistant.

## 5. Naming governance after lock

The following are now prohibited without an explicit documented exception:

1. restarting broad naming cycles;
2. generating replacement master-brand candidates merely because an unrelated use of Vestren exists;
3. reopening rejected Cycle 015–020 candidates as cosmetic variants;
4. changing Vestren because an exact social handle or preferred TLD is unavailable;
5. treating an unrelated fashion, surname, historical, place-name, or entertainment occurrence as an automatic blocker;
6. replacing Vestren with a new candidate without new evidence of a material same-category legal or commercial conflict.

A naming exception may reopen the decision only when new evidence demonstrates a material blocker in the intended commercial category or jurisdiction, or when professional counsel advises that continued use presents unacceptable legal risk.

## 6. Collision doctrine

The project continues to use whole-mark analysis.

Component-level occurrence is not automatically terminal.

Evidence hierarchy:

- C0 — no material collision
- C1 — incidental/unrelated use; document
- C2 — adjacent/component risk; investigate
- C3 — material category conflict; normally reject
- C4 — dominant/confusing identity; reject
- C5 — professional/legal block; reject

For Vestren, unrelated occurrences must not be promoted into a C3–C5 blocker without evidence.

## 7. Professional-clearance boundary

Internal brand lock and legal clearance are distinct states.

The project may proceed with Vestren as its product/master brand while preserving a professional-clearance record. Trademark counsel or qualified clearance professionals remain responsible for final legal conclusions, filing strategy, similarity analysis, and jurisdiction-specific risk.

Future clearance evidence should be appended to the project record rather than used as a reason for continuous naming churn unless a material blocker is established.

## 8. Permanent historical exclusions

Previously terminal candidates remain excluded. This includes Mettle Cove, Trace, Orchard, OrChat, and the other candidates recorded as terminal in the Cycle 015–020 naming history.

They must not be resurrected as alternatives to Vestren through spelling, punctuation, reversal, fusion, suffixes, or cosmetic variants.

## 9. Operational consequence

Naming work is complete.

The project now moves from:

**NAMING → BRAND IMPLEMENTATION → PRODUCT BUILD → VALIDATION → COMMERCIALIZATION**

Future work should prioritize architecture, product, developer experience, security, observability, demand validation, real users, and commercial evidence.

Do not spend additional cycles searching for a theoretically empty word when the approved mark is sufficiently distinctive for its intended category and the project has a credible clearance path.
